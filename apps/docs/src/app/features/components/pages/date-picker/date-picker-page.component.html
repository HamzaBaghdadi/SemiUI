Month/year navigation, Today button, header template

Click the month/year label to drill down into a 12-month grid, then a year list, for jumping further than one click at a time. showTodayButton adds a footer button that jumps to and selects today.

Pick a date

Time picker (12h/24h)

showTime adds hour/minute controls below the calendar -- the picked day and time combine into one Date.

Time-only and manual text entry

timeOnly hides the calendar entirely. manualInput lets you type directly into the field instead of only opening the panel by click.

Multiple dates and range selection

selectionMode="multiple" binds through [(multipleValue)] instead of value. selectionMode="range" binds through [(rangeValue)]. Neither is routed through ngModel/reactive forms/Signal Forms the way the default single mode is.
